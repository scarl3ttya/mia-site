Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

The Mia Bennett Fits publishing tool ("the tool") is a private tool used only by its operator to upload videos to the operator's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

What we collect

When the operator connects a TikTok account, the tool receives the account's TikTok user ID (open_id) and access tokens from TikTok. These are stored only on the operator's own computer and used only to upload videos as drafts to that account.

What we do not do

We do not collect data from other TikTok users, sell or share any data, use data for advertising, or track visitors to this site. This site sets no cookies.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the operator disconnects the account or deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings under security and app permissions.
